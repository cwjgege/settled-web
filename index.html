Questions travelers ask before starting

TripTide is designed for flexible planning before and during a trip.

Is TripTide free to try?

Yes. You can begin as a guest and plan manually. A Single Trip Pass unlocks AI planning for one selected trip, while TripTide Plus supports ongoing planning within the limits shown in the app.

Can TripTide plan from screenshots and booking details?

Yes. Add screenshots, links, notes, and booking materials. TripTide can identify useful trip details and help connect them to places and itinerary items after you approve AI data sharing.

Can I plan a trip with friends?

Yes. Sign in with Apple or Google to use cloud synchronization and collaboration. Trip owners can choose permissions and sharing duration.

Does TripTide replace official travel information?

No. Travel details can change. Confirm bookings, opening hours, visa requirements, and safety information with official sources before you travel.
